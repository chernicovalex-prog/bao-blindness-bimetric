# Release notes — v28.0 public working draft

**Release date:** 1 November 2026  
**Status:** public working draft; methodological audit continues

This release updates the public scientific account from manuscripts
v21.5/v21.6 to v28.0 while preserving all earlier public files. It is an
editorial and methodological-status release, not a claim that a new production
MCMC or full model-selection analysis has been completed.

## Main corrections and qualifications

- profiling is distinguished from marginalisation;
- per-probe profile likelihoods are not described as evidence or Bayes factors;
- arithmetic χ² sums are labelled descriptive rather than a certified joint
  likelihood;
- BAO, SNe, CMB, and Ω_m statements are restricted to their tested domain and
  evidence class;
- the baseline linear/WKB diagnostic is no longer generalised to the death of
  the whole linear sector;
- compressed CMB is explicitly conditional on a GR-calibrated geometric
  convention;
- the unsupported `Δχ²_CMB ≲ 10⁻³` late-ISW/S₈ estimate is withdrawn;
- exploratory posterior material is excluded from the evidentiary basis.

## Reproducibility

Executable v10.6 remains the legacy companion to v21.5/v21.6. Archive v13.3 is
included for documentary inspection. Neither fact is presented as full
one-command reproduction of every v28.0 number. See `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.
