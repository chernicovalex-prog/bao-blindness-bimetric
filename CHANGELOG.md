# Public release history

## v28.0 public working draft — 1 November 2026

- Added the current public working draft in Markdown and PDF.
- Marked the work explicitly as continuing; no final model-selection claim or
  accepted production P3/MCMC posterior is asserted.
- Reframed the scalar-sector result as a baseline linear/WKB diagnostic rather
  than a blanket exclusion of the whole linear sector.
- Corrected the statistical description: `f` and `M` are profiled, not
  marginalised.
- Limited p-values and BIC statements to descriptive per-probe diagnostics;
  removed universal model-selection interpretations from arithmetic χ² sums.
- Removed SNe sigma-preference wording that treated the per-probe diagnostic
  as a full posterior comparison.
- Bounded the Ω_m-rigidity statement to the tested 20-point grid and local
  analytic approximation.
- Clarified that compressed CMB is a conditional GR-calibrated geometric
  diagnostic, not a full bimetric Boltzmann/ISW test.
- Withdrew the unsupported quantitative `Δχ²_CMB ≲ 10⁻³` late-ISW/S₈ bound.
- Removed exploratory posterior material from the evidentiary basis.
- Added version-specific reproduction boundaries in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.
- Retained executable archive v10.6 as legacy and added v13.3 as a documentary
  archive, without claiming full v28.0 reproduction.
- Updated README, citation metadata, release notes, and Zenodo metadata.
