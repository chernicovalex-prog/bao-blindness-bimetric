## Description

This research preview contains the current manuscript, the standalone arXiv source package and the current code/data reproduction package. It is not a final peer-reviewed paper and not a claim of full scientific acceptance.

The work decomposes the Högås–Mörtsell bimetric benchmark across BAO, supernovae and a conditional compressed-CMB background diagnostic, and evaluates the scalar gradient-instability boundary. A good background fit is not treated as proof of viability or model preference.

The GR-calibrated compressed-CMB transfer remains conditional. Perturbation-level CMB validity, nonlinear saturation and a full H&M-convention joint reconstruction are not established here. The H&M joint MAP values and the descriptive per-probe sum are different estimators and are not interchangeable.
